FAQ

Who is this fair for? Can I bring my children and parents?

Anyone interested in pixel-related art, bit music, or game development is at the right place. Pixel Fair welcomes everyone -- youth, adolescents, adults, families -- to explore pixel culture while having fun.

How many events can I register and is there anything I need to bring to certain ones?

You can register for as many events as possible! All events are spaced out, so no two events occur concurrently. All equipment and tools are provided at each event, so just make sure bring yourself~

What are the hours of the fair and where is the fair located?

The fair takes place from 9 AM to 10 PM at Pixel Festival Grounds, 4-8-2 Nijidori, Hikari-machi Sakurabe Prefecture 427-0834, Japan
